🦜 ChromaWing

Genetics Breeding Game - Prototype

How to Play

1. Click on two parrots to select them as parents (they will highlight in blue)

2. Click "Breed Parrots" to create offspring

3. New parrots inherit genes from their parents following Mendelian genetics

4. Watch your collection grow with unique genetic combinations!

Gene Legend

R/r - Red color
RR=Deep, Rr=Bright, rr=None
Y/y - Yellow color
YY=Golden, Yy=Bright, yy=None
B/b - Blue color
BB=Deep, Bb=Sky, bb=None
V/v - Speed
VV=Fast, Vv=Normal, vv=Slow
A/a - Agility
AA=High, Aa=Normal, aa=Low
C/c - Crest
CC/Cc=Has crest, cc=None
