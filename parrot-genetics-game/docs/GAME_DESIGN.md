# CHROMAWING: A Genetics Breeding Game

## Game Concept

**ChromaWing** is a genetics-based breeding simulation game where players breed beautiful parrots (or other creatures) to explore the intersection of genetics, beauty, competition, and perfection. The game challenges players to question: *What is perfect? What is beautiful?*

## Core Philosophy

The game explores several philosophical themes:

1. **Subjective Beauty**: Different competition categories value different traits - what's perfect for speed racing isn't perfect for color shows
2. **The Perfect Specimen**: Players pursue perfection, but discover that perfection is contextual and multi-faceted
3. **Genetic Diversity vs. Specialization**: The tension between breeding specialists and maintaining genetic diversity
4. **Natural vs. Artificial Selection**: How breeding choices shape populations over generations

## Game Overview

Players run a parrot breeding sanctuary where they:
- Breed parrots with various genetic traits
- Compete in different competitive events
- Discover and unlock rare genetic combinations
- Build their reputation and expand their sanctuary
- Trade genetics with other players (or AI breeders)

## Core Gameplay Loop

1. **Breed** - Select parent parrots and produce offspring
2. **Evaluate** - Assess offspring genetics and traits
3. **Train** - Develop performance traits (speed, agility, intelligence)
4. **Compete** - Enter competitions to earn rewards
5. **Expand** - Use rewards to unlock new features, traits, and breeding options
6. **Repeat** - Continue breeding toward specific goals

## Victory Conditions

The game offers multiple paths to "victory":
- **Collector**: Unlock all possible color variations
- **Champion**: Win competitions across all categories
- **Perfectionist**: Breed a 5-star specimen in every trait
- **Geneticist**: Discover all genetic combinations
- **Naturalist**: Maintain the most diverse gene pool
- **Specialist**: Create the ultimate specimen in one specific category

## Target Audience

- Genetics enthusiasts
- Pokemon/breeding game fans
- Strategy gamers
- Educational gaming (biology/genetics concepts)
- Casual players who enjoy collection and progression

## Platform Considerations

- **Web-based**: Accessible, easy to share results
- **Mobile-friendly**: Touch controls for breeding selection
- **Potential for multiplayer**: Trading, competitions, leaderboards
