# Gameplay Mechanics

## Competition System

Competitions are the primary way players test their parrots and earn rewards. Different competitions value different traits, making "perfection" contextual.

### Competition Categories

#### 1. Beauty Pageants
**What matters**: Color rarity, pattern beauty, feature harmony

**Judging Criteria**:
- **Color Vibrancy** (30%): Intensity and saturation of colors
- **Color Harmony** (25%): How well colors complement each other
- **Pattern Appeal** (20%): Uniqueness and aesthetic of patterns
- **Feature Balance** (15%): Proportions of crest, tail, size
- **Rarity Bonus** (10%): Extra points for rare genetic combinations

**Ideal Traits**: High intensity gene, rare color combinations, elaborate features

**Player Choice**: Which beauty category to enter:
- Classic Beauty (traditional color combinations)
- Exotic Beauty (rare, unusual combinations)
- Natural Beauty (wild-type appearances)

#### 2. Speed Racing
**What matters**: Velocity, wing shape, size

**Race Mechanics**:
- Timed flight courses
- Straight racing (pure speed)
- Sprint races (acceleration important)
- Endurance races (stamina important)

**Performance Formula**:
```
Race Time = Base Time × Speed Modifier × Size Modifier × Wing Modifier × Stamina Factor
```

**Ideal Traits**: VV (fast), ss (small, agile), ww (narrow wings for speed), EE (endurance)

#### 3. Agility Courses
**What matters**: Agility, size, intelligence

**Course Mechanics**:
- Obstacle navigation
- Tight turns and maneuvers
- Precision flying
- Timed completion with accuracy scoring

**Performance Formula**:
```
Score = (Time Bonus × Agility) + (Accuracy × Intelligence) - (Size Penalty)
```

**Ideal Traits**: AA (high agility), ss (small), NN (intelligent), ww (narrow wings)

#### 4. Intelligence Challenges
**What matters**: Intelligence, patience (linked to stamina)

**Challenge Types**:
- Puzzle solving
- Trick learning
- Pattern recognition
- Memory games

**Ideal Traits**: NN (high intelligence), EE (patience/stamina)

#### 5. Endurance Trials
**What matters**: Stamina, size, wing shape

**Trial Format**:
- Long-distance flights
- Multiple events in succession
- Sustained performance over time

**Ideal Traits**: EE (endurance), WW (broad wings), SS (large size for staying power)

#### 6. All-Around Competition
**What matters**: Balance across all traits

**Scoring**: Weighted average across multiple mini-events
- Tests versatility rather than specialization
- Hardest to win, highest prestige

### Competition Tiers

Competitions are organized by difficulty/prestige:

1. **Local Events** (Tier 1)
   - Entry cost: Low
   - Rewards: Small
   - Requirements: Any parrot
   - Good for beginners and testing

2. **Regional Championships** (Tier 2)
   - Entry cost: Medium
   - Rewards: Medium
   - Requirements: 2-star minimum in relevant category
   - More competitive

3. **National Tournaments** (Tier 3)
   - Entry cost: High
   - Rewards: Large
   - Requirements: 3-star minimum
   - Serious competition

4. **World Championships** (Tier 4)
   - Entry cost: Very High
   - Rewards: Massive
   - Requirements: 4-star minimum
   - Elite competition

5. **Grand Masters** (Tier 5)
   - Entry cost: Premium
   - Rewards: Legendary
   - Requirements: 5-star, proven track record
   - Ultimate challenge

### Rewards System

Competitions award:
- **Credits**: Game currency for purchases
- **Reputation**: Unlocks new features and breeding options
- **Genetic Samples**: Rare genes from champion lineages
- **Trophies**: Cosmetic collectibles and achievements
- **Breeding Rights**: Access to champion studs for breeding

## Resource Management

### Primary Resources

#### 1. Credits (Currency)
- Earned from competitions, selling offspring, quests
- Spent on: breeding, training, facility upgrades, items

#### 2. Energy
- Parrots have limited energy
- Depleted by: breeding, competing, training
- Regenerates over time or with items
- Forces strategic choices about which activities to prioritize

#### 3. Space
- Limited habitat space
- Must expand to house more parrots
- Creates pressure to sell/release less desirable offspring
- Strategic decisions about breeding population

#### 4. Food/Care
- Daily upkeep for parrots
- Higher-tier parrots cost more to maintain
- Balancing act between quality and quantity

#### 5. Reputation
- Earned through competition success
- Unlocks: new genes, facilities, breeding partners, events
- Acts as progression gate

### Time Management

#### Daily Activities
- Feed and care for parrots (quick)
- Training sessions (medium time)
- Breeding selection (strategic, take your time)
- Competitions (various durations)

#### Energy System
- Each parrot has daily energy
- Competing uses energy
- Breeding uses energy
- Training uses energy
- Creates meaningful choices about parrot usage

## Breeding Mechanics

### Breeding Process

1. **Select Parents**
   - View genetic compatibility
   - See prediction range of possible offspring
   - Cost varies based on parent quality

2. **Breeding Time**
   - Eggs take time to hatch (real-time or accelerated)
   - Can speed up with premium items
   - Anticipation builds excitement

3. **Hatching**
   - Reveal offspring genetics
   - Visual "reveal" animation
   - Immediate evaluation of traits

4. **Naming and Decision**
   - Keep for breeding/competing
   - Sell for credits
   - Release to wild (minimal return, roleplay option)

### Breeding Strategies

**Line Breeding**: Breed related parrots to reinforce desired traits
- Risk: Inbreeding penalties (reduced stamina, health issues)
- Reward: Predictable, consistent results

**Outcrossing**: Breed unrelated parrots for diversity
- Risk: Unpredictable results
- Reward: Genetic diversity, potential for rare combinations

**Selective Breeding**: Multi-generation project targeting specific genes
- Requires planning and patience
- Most reliable way to create "perfect" specimens

### Breeding Predictions

The game shows probability ranges:
```
Parent 1: Rr Yy Bb
Parent 2: RR Yy bb

Offspring Predictions:
Red Gene: 50% RR, 50% Rr (100% will show red)
Yellow Gene: 25% YY, 50% Yy, 25% yy (75% will show yellow)
Blue Gene: 50% Bb, 50% bb (50% chance of blue)

Possible Color Outcomes:
- Red/Orange (red + yellow): 75%
- Red only: 25%
- Small chance of blue tints: 50%
```

## Training System

### Training Mechanics

Parrots can be trained to improve performance stats (within genetic limits):
- **Base stats** determined by genetics
- **Training** can add +20% bonus to base stats
- **Training takes time and energy**
- **Diminishing returns** as stats approach cap

### Training Activities

**Speed Training**: Flight drills, improve acceleration
**Agility Training**: Obstacle courses, improve maneuverability
**Intelligence Training**: Puzzle games, trick learning
**Endurance Training**: Long flights, improve stamina

### Training Limits

```
Max Stat = Base Stat (from genes) × 1.2

Example:
- Base Speed: 50 (from genes)
- Max Trainable: 60
- Current Training: 55
```

This ensures genetics still matter while allowing some customization.

## Beauty and Perfection: The Philosophical Question

### Multiple Definitions of "Perfect"

The game deliberately presents different, sometimes conflicting, standards:

**Speed Perfect**: VV ss ww EE (but might look unremarkable)
**Beauty Perfect**: Rare color combination, II, elaborate features (but might be slow)
**Balanced Perfect**: All stats 3+ stars (jack of all trades, master of none)
**Rare Perfect**: Legendary genetic combination (collectible, might not perform well)

### Player Choice and Values

Players must decide what they value:
- Competitive performance?
- Aesthetic beauty?
- Rarity collection?
- Genetic purity?
- Diversity?

There is no single "correct" answer, encouraging diverse playstyles and personal goals.

### Commentary Through Gameplay

The game subtly explores themes:
- "Perfect" depends on context and criteria
- Beauty is subjective and culturally defined
- Specialization vs. generalization tradeoffs
- Natural diversity vs. artificial selection
- The ethics and appeal of genetic manipulation

Players naturally encounter these questions through gameplay without heavy-handed messaging.
