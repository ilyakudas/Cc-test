# Quick Reference Guide

## Gene Notation

### Understanding Alleles
- **Uppercase (R, Y, B)** = Dominant allele
- **Lowercase (r, y, b)** = Recessive allele

### Genotype Patterns
- **RR** = Homozygous dominant (two dominant alleles)
- **Rr** = Heterozygous (one of each)
- **rr** = Homozygous recessive (two recessive alleles)

## Breeding Predictions

### Basic Inheritance Formula

```
Parent 1: Aa
Parent 2: Aa

Offspring possibilities:
- AA: 25% (1/4)
- Aa: 50% (2/4)
- aa: 25% (1/4)
```

### Quick Probability Rules

**Two Heterozygous (Aa × Aa)**
→ 75% dominant trait, 25% recessive

**Homozygous × Heterozygous (AA × Aa)**
→ 100% dominant trait (50% AA, 50% Aa)

**Homozygous × Homozygous (AA × aa)**
→ 100% heterozygous (Aa)

**Two Recessive (aa × aa)**
→ 100% recessive trait

## Breeding Strategies

### Line Breeding
**Goal**: Reinforce specific traits
**Method**: Breed related parrots (parent-offspring, siblings)
**Risk**: Inbreeding penalties
**Best For**: Creating specialists

### Outcrossing
**Goal**: Genetic diversity
**Method**: Breed unrelated parrots
**Risk**: Unpredictable results
**Best For**: Discovering new combinations

### Selective Breeding
**Goal**: Multi-generation projects
**Method**: Carefully select traits over 3-5 generations
**Best For**: Creating "perfect" specimens

### Hybrid Vigor
**Goal**: Balanced excellence
**Method**: Cross different specialist lines
**Best For**: All-around competitors

## Training Caps

**Maximum Training Bonus**: +20% of base stat

Example:
- Base Speed: 50 (from genes)
- Max Trainable: 60 (50 × 1.2)
- Can add: +10 through training

**Training is percentage-based, not replacement!**

## Mutation Chances

- **Base Rate**: 0.5-2% per breeding
- **Types**:
  - Beneficial (new colors, enhanced traits)
  - Neutral (cosmetic changes)
  - Detrimental (reduced performance)

## Quick Tips

1. **Start Simple**: Master basic colors before complex genetics
2. **Diversify Early**: Collect varied genetics before specializing
3. **Plan Ahead**: Think 2-3 generations ahead for breeding projects
4. **Save Champions**: Never sell a competition winner
5. **Track Lineages**: Name parrots systematically to remember genetics
6. **Energy Efficiency**: Don't waste energy on low-tier competitions late game
7. **Train Smart**: Focus training on near-threshold parrots (e.g., 88 → 92)
8. **Rarity ≠ Quality**: A common parrot can still be a champion

## Glossary

**Allele**: One version of a gene (R or r)
**Dominant**: Allele that expresses when present (uppercase)
**Recessive**: Allele that only expresses when paired (lowercase)
**Genotype**: Genetic makeup (what genes it has)
**Phenotype**: Expressed traits (how it looks/performs)
**Homozygous**: Two identical alleles (RR or rr)
**Heterozygous**: Two different alleles (Rr)
**Punnett Square**: Diagram showing breeding probabilities
**Line Breeding**: Breeding related individuals
**Outcrossing**: Breeding unrelated individuals
