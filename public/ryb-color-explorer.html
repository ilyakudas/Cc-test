📊 RYB Genetics System Explained

Traditional Artist's Color Wheel:

RYB (Red-Yellow-Blue) is the traditional color model used in art education and painting. Unlike RGB (used in screens), RYB represents how pigments mix in the physical world.

Primary Colors (Pure pigments):

  • Red (0°): Cannot be created by mixing other colors
  • Yellow (120°): Cannot be created by mixing other colors
  • Blue (240°): Cannot be created by mixing other colors

Secondary Colors (Mix two primaries):

  • Orange (60°): Red + Yellow
  • Green (180°): Yellow + Blue
  • Purple (300°): Red + Blue

Complementary Colors (Opposite on wheel):

  • Red ↔ Green: Maximum contrast, creates visual excitement
  • Yellow ↔ Purple: Warm vs cool balance
  • Blue ↔ Orange: Cool vs warm harmony

How Genes Work (Subtractive Mixing):

Each body part has 6 genes for each color channel (Red, Yellow, Blue). The COUNT of dominant alleles determines pigment intensity (0-6 dominant = 0%-100% pigment). Important: This uses subtractive mixing like real paints!

  • rrrrrr yyyyyy bbbbbb = White (no pigment, paper shows through)
  • RRRRRR yyyyyy bbbbbb = Red (100% red pigment only)
  • RRRRRR YYYYYY bbbbbb = Orange (red + yellow pigments mix)
  • rrrrrr YYYYYY BBBBBB = Green (yellow + blue pigments mix)
  • RRRRRR yyyyyy BBBBBB = Purple (red + blue pigments mix)
  • RRRrrr YYYrrr BBBrrr = Grey (equal amounts of all pigments = 50% grey)
  • RRRRRR YYYYYY BBBBBB = Black (all pigments mixed = dark/black)

8 Pattern Types (controlled by genes P1, P2, P3):

  • 000 - Solid: All 6 genes work together for uniform color
  • 001 - Horizontal 2-Color: Genes 1-3 = left, genes 4-6 = right
  • 010 - Vertical 2-Color: Genes 1-3 = top, genes 4-6 = bottom
  • 011 - Radial 2-Color: Genes 1-3 = center, genes 4-6 = edge
  • 100 - Horizontal 3-Color: Genes 1-2, 3-4, 5-6 = three horizontal bands
  • 101 - Vertical 3-Color: Genes 1-2, 3-4, 5-6 = three vertical bands
  • 110 - Diagonal: Genes 1-3 = top-left, genes 4-6 = bottom-right
  • 111 - Bilateral Mirror: Genes 1-3 = edges, genes 4-6 = center (mirrored)

4 Animation Types (controlled by genes P4, P5):

  • 00 - Still: No animation, static display
  • 01 - Color Shift: Colors shift through subtle hue variations
  • 10 - Slide: Gradient position moves across the feathers
  • 11 - Shimmer: Wave of light sweeps across with color brightening
