## Risk Management

### High-Risk Areas

**Genetics Complexity**
- *Risk*: System too complex for players
- *Mitigation*: Extensive playtesting, good tutorial, visual aids

**Balance Issues**
- *Risk*: Dominant strategies, uncompetitive builds
- *Mitigation*: Multiple victory paths, continuous balancing

**Performance**
- *Risk*: Slow with many parrots
- *Mitigation*: Optimization early, caching, lazy loading

**Scope Creep**
- *Risk*: Too many features, delayed launch
- *Mitigation*: Strict MVP definition, phased releases

### Contingency Plans

**If Behind Schedule:**
1. Cut P2/P3 features
2. Simplify visual rendering
3. Reduce initial gene variety
4. Postpone multiplayer

**If Over Budget:**
1. Use free/open-source assets
2. Procedural generation over custom art
3. Solo development over team
4. Phased paid releases

## Conclusion

This roadmap provides a structured path from concept to launch. The phased approach allows for:
- **Incremental development** - Build and test iteratively
- **Risk mitigation** - Identify issues early
- **Flexibility** - Adapt based on feedback
- **Sustainable pace** - Avoid burnout

The project is ambitious but achievable with proper planning and execution. Focus on the MVP first, then expand based on player feedback and available resources.
