📊 Genetics System Explained

Normal Distribution:

With 6 genes per color channel, there are different numbers of ways to get each count:

  • 0 dominant (rrrrrr): 1 way → 0% color (1.6%)
  • 1 dominant (Rrrrr): 6 ways → 17% color (9.4%)
  • 2 dominant (RRrrrr): 15 ways → 33% color (23.4%)
  • 3 dominant (RRRrrr): 20 ways → 50% color (most common! 31.3%)
  • 4 dominant (RRRRrr): 15 ways → 67% color (23.4%)
  • 5 dominant (RRRRRr): 6 ways → 83% color (9.4%)
  • 6 dominant (RRRRRR): 1 way → 100% color (1.6%)

This creates realistic variation: Most offspring will have medium colors (around 50%), with fewer pure/absent colors.

Prevents graying: Each body part has independent genes, so wings can be pure red while body is pure green!

8 Pattern Types (controlled by genes P1, P2, P3):

  • 000 - Solid: All 6 genes work together for uniform color
  • 001 - Horizontal 2-Color: Genes 1-3 = left, genes 4-6 = right
  • 010 - Vertical 2-Color: Genes 1-3 = top, genes 4-6 = bottom
  • 011 - Radial 2-Color: Genes 1-3 = center, genes 4-6 = edge
  • 100 - Horizontal 3-Color: Genes 1-2, 3-4, 5-6 = three horizontal bands
  • 101 - Vertical 3-Color: Genes 1-2, 3-4, 5-6 = three vertical bands
  • 110 - Diagonal: Genes 1-3 = top-left, genes 4-6 = bottom-right
  • 111 - Bilateral Mirror: Genes 1-3 = edges, genes 4-6 = center (mirrored)

4 Animation Types (controlled by genes P4, P5):

  • 00 - Still: No animation, static display
  • 01 - Color Shift: Colors shift through subtle hue variations while maintaining original color recognizability
  • 10 - Slide: Gradient position moves across the feathers
  • 11 - Shimmer: Wave of light sweeps across with color brightening
