📊 Genetics Information

How It Works:

Each parrot has genes that control different aspects of its coloration. The genes follow Mendelian inheritance patterns:

  • Dominant alleles (uppercase) express even with one copy
  • Recessive alleles (lowercase) require two copies to express
  • Colors can mix: Yellow + Blue = Green, Red + Yellow = Orange
  • Intensity affects saturation: II = vibrant, Ii = normal, ii = pale

Common Combinations:

  • Scarlet Macaw: RR YY bb - Red and yellow create orange-red
  • Blue-and-Gold: rr YY BB - Yellow body with blue wings
  • Emerald: rr YY BB - Yellow and blue mix to green
  • Rainbow: RR YY BB - All three colors present
