📊 Gene Mapping System

Body Part to Gene Mapping:

  • Wings: Use genes 1-2 (r1, r2, g1, g2, b1, b2) - max 4 dominant alleles per channel
  • Body: Use genes 3-4 (r3, r4, g3, g4, b3, b4) - max 4 dominant alleles per channel
  • Head/Crest: Use genes 1,3 (r1, r3, g1, g3, b1, b3) - blend of wing and body genes
  • Tail: Use genes 2,4 (r2, r4, g2, g4, b2, b4) - complementary blend
  • Accents: Use all genes (r1-r4, g1-g4, b1-b4) - full genetic expression

Why This Prevents Graying:

By mapping different body parts to different gene subsets, each part can have its own distinct color. For example: Wings can be pure red (RR RR gg gg bb bb) while Body is pure green (rr rr GG GG bb bb), creating a beautiful red-and-green parrot instead of a gray one!
