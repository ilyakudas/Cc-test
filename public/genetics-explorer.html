📊 Genetics System Explained

Normal Distribution:

With 4 genes per color channel, there are different numbers of ways to get each count:

  • 0 dominant (rrrr): 1 way → 0% color
  • 1 dominant (Rrrr): 4 ways → 25% color
  • 2 dominant (RRrr): 6 ways → 50% color (most common!)
  • 3 dominant (RRRr): 4 ways → 75% color
  • 4 dominant (RRRR): 1 way → 100% color

This creates realistic variation: Most offspring will have medium colors (around 50%), with fewer pure/absent colors.

Prevents graying: Each body part has independent genes, so wings can be pure red while body is pure green!
