# Creatures and Flora of Kepler-442b

## Fauna Categories

### Passive/Neutral Creatures

#### 1. Lumiskippers
**Type**: Small herbivore
**Description**: Rabbit-sized creatures with six legs and bioluminescent spots
**Behavior**:
- Travel in small groups
- Flee when approached
- Active during twilight
**Resources**: Meat, hide, luminous glands
**Habitat**: Prismatic Plains, forest edges
**Danger Level**: None

#### 2. Prismatic Striders
**Type**: Large herbivore
**Description**: Giraffe-like creatures with crystalline horns that refract light
**Behavior**:
- Peaceful grazers in herds
- Use horns to absorb solar energy
- Migrate seasonally
**Resources**: Large meat portions, crystal horn, tough hide
**Habitat**: Prismatic Plains, Crystalline Forests
**Danger Level**: Low (defensive if provoked)

#### 3. Shell-Backs
**Type**: Armored herbivore
**Description**: Turtle-like creatures the size of bears with metallic shells
**Behavior**:
- Slow-moving
- Retreat into shells when threatened
- Live for centuries
**Resources**: Shell plates (excellent armor material), meat
**Habitat**: Rocky areas, caves
**Danger Level**: None

#### 4. Harmony Birds
**Type**: Flying creature
**Description**: Iridescent avians with four wings that sing in complex harmonies
**Behavior**:
- Nest in crystal trees
- Warning calls alert all nearby creatures
- Collect shiny objects
**Resources**: Feathers, eggs (rare)
**Habitat**: Crystalline Forests, Floating Islands
**Danger Level**: None

### Aggressive Predators

#### 5. Razor-Maws
**Type**: Pack hunter
**Description**: Wolf-sized predators with blade-like mandibles
**Behavior**:
- Hunt in packs of 4-8
- Intelligent pack tactics
- Mark territory aggressively
**Combat**:
- Fast melee attacks
- Can pounce from distance
- Weak to fire
**Resources**: Teeth (crafting material), hide, meat
**Habitat**: Most biomes
**Danger Level**: High

#### 6. Spine-Crawlers
**Type**: Ambush predator
**Description**: Insectoid creatures that camouflage as rocks
**Behavior**:
- Wait for prey to approach
- Shoot poisonous spines
- Retreat if discovered early
**Combat**:
- Ranged poison attacks
- Weak armor on underside
- Slow movement
**Resources**: Poison glands, chitin plates
**Habitat**: Rocky areas, caves, ruins
**Danger Level**: Medium

#### 7. Dune Wyrms
**Type**: Mega predator
**Description**: Massive sandworm-like creatures, 50+ meters long
**Behavior**:
- Sense vibrations on sand
- Swallow prey whole
- Territorial
**Combat**:
- Avoid by staying on rocks
- Vulnerable during breach
- Extreme danger in direct combat
**Resources**: Wyrm scales, acid glands, huge meat yield
**Habitat**: Crimson Dunes exclusively
**Danger Level**: Extreme

#### 8. Void Stalkers
**Type**: Nocturnal apex predator
**Description**: Shadowy feline creatures that seem to absorb light
**Behavior**:
- Only active at night
- Near-invisible in darkness
- Solitary hunters
**Combat**:
- Stealth attacks
- Extremely fast
- Weak to light sources
**Resources**: Shadow pelt (stealth armor), rare organs
**Habitat**: All biomes at night
**Danger Level**: Extreme

#### 9. Plasma Jellies
**Type**: Floating predator
**Description**: Jellyfish-like creatures that float on air currents, trailing electric tentacles
**Behavior**:
- Drift on winds
- Shocking touch
- Drawn to electrical storms
**Combat**:
- Electric attacks
- Float out of melee range
- Vulnerable to projectiles
**Resources**: Plasma cores, membrane (insulation)
**Habitat**: Storm Belt, Floating Islands
**Danger Level**: High

### Special/Rare Creatures

#### 10. Crystal Golems
**Type**: Living mineral
**Description**: Walking formations of crystal and stone
**Behavior**:
- Extremely rare
- Protect crystal forests
- Nearly immortal
**Combat**:
- Massive damage
- Nearly invulnerable
- Very slow
- Can be befriended with offerings
**Resources**: Pure crystals, heart stone
**Habitat**: Crystalline Forests
**Danger Level**: Extreme (unless befriended)

#### 11. Echo Wraiths
**Type**: Energy beings
**Description**: Ghostly creatures made of sound and light
**Behavior**:
- Appear near ancient ruins
- Non-hostile unless ruins are disturbed
- Can pass through solid matter
**Combat**:
- Energy drain attacks
- Immune to physical damage
- Vulnerable to sound weapons
**Resources**: Echo essence (advanced crafting)
**Habitat**: Ancient Ruins Complex
**Danger Level**: High

#### 12. Tide Leviathan
**Type**: Aquatic mega-fauna
**Description**: Whale-sized creature in coastal and ocean areas
**Behavior**:
- Generally peaceful
- Surfaces during high tide
- Ancient and intelligent
**Combat**:
- Only hostile if attacked
- Creates massive waves
- Near-impossible to kill
**Resources**: Leviathan blubber, bones (if naturally deceased)
**Habitat**: Shattered Coast, Subterranean Ocean
**Danger Level**: Extreme (if provoked)

## Flora

### Resource Plants

#### Nutrition Pods
**Description**: Bulbous plants with edible fruit-like growths
**Uses**: Food source (must be cooked)
**Location**: Prismatic Plains
**Harvest**: Cut pods carefully

#### Fiber Reeds
**Description**: Tall grass-like plants with tough fibers
**Uses**: Rope, cloth, basic construction
**Location**: Widespread
**Harvest**: Cut at base

#### Aloe Crystals
**Description**: Succulent-like plant with crystalline gel
**Uses**: Healing salve, hydration
**Location**: Desert and dry areas
**Harvest**: Break leaf, collect gel

### Crafting Materials

#### Resonance Flowers
**Description**: Flowers that vibrate at specific frequencies
**Uses**: Electronic components, sensors
**Location**: Crystalline Forests
**Harvest**: Delicate extraction required

#### Thorn Vines
**Description**: Aggressive climbing plants with metal-hard thorns
**Uses**: Weapons, defensive barriers
**Location**: Toxic Marshlands
**Harvest**: Cut with proper tools

#### Ghost Wood
**Description**: Pale, lightweight wood from crystal trees
**Uses**: Construction, insulation
**Location**: Crystalline Forests
**Harvest**: Dead trees only (living ones protected by golems)

### Dangerous Plants

#### Snap Pods
**Description**: Plant pods that explode when disturbed
**Behavior**:
- Seed dispersal mechanism
- Creates shrapnel burst
- Can chain react
**Avoidance**: Wide berth, shoot from distance
**Uses**: Can be harvested for explosives (dangerous)
**Location**: Most biomes

#### Creeping Moss
**Description**: Carnivorous moss that slowly envelops creatures
**Behavior**:
- Appears harmless initially
- Secretes paralytic toxin
- Slowly digests prey
**Avoidance**: Watch for bones in moss patches
**Uses**: Toxin extraction (medicinal and poison)
**Location**: Caves, shaded areas

#### Mind Spores
**Description**: Fungal clouds that cause hallucinations
**Behavior**:
- Release spores when disturbed
- Causes disorientation and visions
- Can be fatal in large doses
**Avoidance**: Filter mask required
**Uses**: Medicinal (small doses), chemical research
**Location**: Caves, Toxic Marshlands

### Symbiotic/Useful Plants

#### Lamp Lichen
**Description**: Bioluminescent lichen that sticks to surfaces
**Uses**: Natural lighting for bases
**Location**: Caves
**Cultivation**: Can be transplanted and grown

#### Air Purifiers
**Description**: Large flower that filters toxins from air
**Uses**: Base atmosphere improvement
**Location**: Various biomes
**Cultivation**: Difficult but valuable

#### Solar Caps
**Description**: Mushrooms that store solar energy
**Uses**: Biofuel, energy source
**Location**: Sunny areas
**Cultivation**: Can be farmed with proper conditions

## Ecosystem Interactions

### Food Chain
```
Plants → Herbivores (Lumiskippers, Striders, Shell-Backs)
       → Small Predators (Razor-Maws)
       → Apex Predators (Void Stalkers, Dune Wyrms)
```

### Symbiotic Relationships
- Harmony Birds nest in crystal trees, protect them from parasites
- Shell-Backs eat Creeping Moss, keeping it in check
- Prismatic Striders pollinate Resonance Flowers

### Player Integration
- Players can disrupt ecosystems by overhunting
- Creating farms affects nearby wildlife
- Some creatures can be tamed or befriended
- Understanding ecosystem is key to survival

## Behavioral Patterns

### Daily Cycles
- Diurnal: Striders, Lumiskippers, Harmony Birds
- Nocturnal: Void Stalkers, Echo Wraiths, certain insects
- Crepuscular: Razor-Maws (dawn and dusk hunters)

### Seasonal Changes
- Spore season: Many creatures migrate or hibernate
- Wet season: Aquatic creatures more active
- Dry season: Concentration around water sources

### Territorial Behaviors
- Razor-Maws mark territory with scent
- Crystal Golems patrol specific groves
- Dune Wyrms claim sections of desert
