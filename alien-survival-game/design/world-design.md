# World Design: Planet Kepler-442b

## Planet Overview

**Name**: Kepler-442b (nicknamed "The Prism" by colonists)
**Type**: Super-Earth
**Size**: 1.6x Earth's radius
**Gravity**: 1.3x Earth gravity
**Atmosphere**: Breathable but with toxic pockets
**Day Length**: 32 hours (20 hours daylight, 12 hours night)
**Moons**: 2 (one large, one small - creates unique tidal effects)
**Star System**: Binary star system (orange dwarf + red dwarf)

## Environmental Features

### Atmospheric Composition
- 65% Nitrogen
- 25% Oxygen
- 8% Xenon (causes aurora effects)
- 2% Trace gases (some toxic)
- Pollen/spores seasonal

### Climate Zones
1. **Temperate Equator**: Starting area, relatively safe
2. **Tropical Acidlands**: High rainfall, corrosive water
3. **Crystal Tundra**: Cold, beautiful, resource-rich
4. **Volcanic Wastes**: Extreme heat, rare minerals
5. **Storm Belt**: Perpetual electrical storms

## Biomes

### 1. Crystalline Forests
**Description**: Towering crystal trees that resonate with sound, creating ethereal music
**Features**:
- Trees grow geometric crystals instead of leaves
- Different crystals produce different tones in wind
- Ground covered in luminous moss
- Natural caves within crystal formations
**Resources**: Resonance crystals, pure water, crystal wood
**Dangers**: Crystal shards fall during storms, territorial creatures
**Atmosphere**: Mystical, serene, alien beauty

### 2. Bioluminescent Caverns
**Description**: Vast underground networks glowing with alien life
**Features**:
- Walls covered in glowing fungi and lichen
- Underground rivers and lakes
- Massive cavern ecosystems
- Ancient ruins hidden in depths
**Resources**: Bioluminescent material, rare minerals, cave water
**Dangers**: Oxygen depletion, cave-ins, blind predators
**Atmosphere**: Mysterious, claustrophobic yet beautiful

### 3. Floating Islands
**Description**: Massive rock formations suspended by magnetic fields
**Features**:
- Anti-gravity crystals keep islands aloft
- Unique fauna evolved in isolation
- Waterfalls that fall into clouds
- Ancient alien observatories
**Resources**: Anti-grav crystals, rare plants, ancient tech
**Dangers**: Falling, unstable platforms, flying predators
**Atmosphere**: Breathtaking, vertigo-inducing, wonder

### 4. Toxic Marshlands
**Description**: Swampy lowlands with acidic water and poisonous gases
**Features**:
- Bubbling pools of chemicals
- Twisted vegetation adapted to toxins
- Gas vents that ignite randomly
- Sunken structures and machinery
**Resources**: Chemical compounds, medicinal plants, salvage
**Dangers**: Acid damage, toxic gas, amphibious predators
**Atmosphere**: Oppressive, dangerous, alien

### 5. Crimson Dunes
**Description**: Vast red desert of iron oxide sand under dual suns
**Features**:
- Massive sandworms create the dunes
- Magnetic anomalies affect equipment
- Ancient fossilized mega-fauna
- Occasional oases with unique ecosystems
**Resources**: Minerals, fossils, glass from lightning strikes
**Dangers**: Extreme heat, sandstorms, massive predators
**Atmosphere**: Desolate, harsh, primordial

### 6. Prismatic Plains
**Description**: Grasslands of multi-colored vegetation that shifts with light
**Features**:
- Plants photosynthesize dual-star light differently
- Creates rainbow effect across landscape
- Herds of megafauna migrate seasonally
- Gentle rolling hills with scattered groves
**Resources**: Plant fibers, game, fruits, clear sightlines
**Dangers**: Stampedes, pack hunters, exposure
**Atmosphere**: Beautiful, open, frontier-like

### 7. The Shattered Coast
**Description**: Coastline of an alien ocean with extreme tides from two moons
**Features**:
- Tide swings of 50+ meters twice daily
- Exposed tidal pools with unique life
- Crashed ships and wreckage
- Storm surge barriers (natural and artificial)
**Resources**: Seafood, salt, salvage, pearls
**Dangers**: Tides, sea creatures, storms
**Atmosphere**: Dynamic, powerful, mysterious

### 8. Ancient Ruins Complex
**Description**: Massive alien city, abandoned for millennia
**Features**:
- Architecture defying human understanding
- Still-functioning alien technology
- Hieroglyphs and holographic records
- Central spire visible from anywhere
**Resources**: Advanced tech, power sources, knowledge
**Dangers**: Security systems, structural collapse, energy anomalies
**Atmosphere**: Awe-inspiring, eerie, profound

## Points of Interest

### Your Crash Site
- Starting location
- Salvageable ship components
- Emergency supplies (limited)
- Tutorial area

### Derelict Colony Ship "Hope's Dawn"
- Previous colonization attempt
- Logs revealing what went wrong
- Advanced supplies and equipment
- Hostile security drones still active

### The Monolith Fields
- Hundreds of black monoliths in perfect grid
- Unknown purpose
- Area of intense research interest
- Strange effects on technology and biology

### Subterranean Ocean
- Massive body of water beneath crust
- Accessible through cave systems
- Unique ecosystem completely independent
- Ancient structures on ocean floor

### The Singing Spire
- Tallest structure on planet
- Alien construction at planet's magnetic pole
- Creates haunting sounds during storms
- May be key to understanding planet's history

## Weather Phenomena

### Spore Season
- Planet-wide event every 200 days
- Reduces visibility to near-zero
- Some spores beneficial, others harmful
- Wildlife becomes more aggressive

### Electrical Super-Storms
- Lightning strikes every few seconds
- Dangerous but can power technology
- Creates temporary portals between biomes
- Produces valuable charged crystals

### The Alignment
- When both stars and moons align
- Extreme tidal forces
- Gravitational anomalies
- Ancient machines activate

## Environmental Hazards

1. **Radiation Zones**: Areas near crashed reactor cores
2. **Quicksand**: In marshlands and certain cave areas
3. **Geysers**: Explosive steam vents in volcanic areas
4. **Acid Rain**: Corrosive precipitation in certain regions
5. **Gravity Wells**: Magnetic anomalies that affect movement
6. **Temporal Distortions**: Near ancient alien tech
