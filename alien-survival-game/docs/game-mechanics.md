# Game Mechanics

## Core Survival Systems

### 1. Resource Management

**Health**
- Damaged by creature attacks, falls, environmental hazards
- Restored by crafted medical supplies, alien plants (risky), rest

**Hunger**
- Depletes over time
- Find edible alien fauna/flora (requires scanning)
- Craft food from harvested materials
- Starvation causes health loss

**Thirst**
- Depletes faster than hunger
- Find water sources (may need purification)
- Collect morning dew, extract from plants
- Dehydration affects stamina and health

**Oxygen**
- Certain biomes have toxic atmosphere
- Craft oxygen filters and tanks
- Some caves require oxygen management
- Plants can provide temporary oxygen boosts

**Stamina**
- Used for sprinting, climbing, combat
- Regenerates during rest
- Affected by hunger, thirst, and weather

### 2. Crafting System

**Tiers**
1. **Primitive**: Basic tools from rocks, sticks, and salvage
2. **Hybrid**: Combining alien materials with human technology
3. **Advanced**: Reverse-engineered alien tech
4. **Exotic**: Mysterious ancient technology

**Crafting Stations**
- Campfire: Basic cooking and primitive crafting
- Workbench: Advanced tools and weapons
- Research Station: Analyze samples, unlock blueprints
- Fabricator: High-tech equipment (late game)

**Key Craftable Items**
- Tools: Multi-tool, scanner, mining laser
- Weapons: Spear, bow, energy weapons
- Shelter: Tents, base modules, defenses
- Equipment: Backpacks, oxygen gear, environmental suits

### 3. Base Building

**Components**
- Foundation: Establish secure areas
- Walls & Roofs: Protection from weather and creatures
- Storage: Organize resources
- Utilities: Water purifier, energy generator, crafting stations
- Defenses: Fences, turrets, alarm systems

**Base Features**
- Modular construction system
- Power management (solar, bio-fuel, alien crystals)
- Expandable as resources allow
- Multiple base locations possible

### 4. Exploration & Discovery

**Scanning System**
- Scan creatures to learn behavior, weaknesses
- Scan plants for edibility, resources, properties
- Scan terrain for minerals and points of interest
- Complete database unlocks bonuses

**Biomes to Explore**
- Crystalline Forests
- Toxic Swamps
- Bioluminescent Caves
- Acid Rain Valleys
- Ancient Ruins
- Floating Islands
- Underground Ocean

**Points of Interest**
- Crash sites (yours and others)
- Alien structures
- Resource-rich areas
- Mysterious artifacts
- Environmental storytelling locations

### 5. Combat System

**Approaches**
- Stealth: Avoid detection, use environment
- Ranged: Bows, thrown weapons, energy weapons
- Melee: Spears, crafted blades, tools
- Traps: Set defenses around base or paths
- Taming: Befriend certain creatures (advanced)

**Enemy AI**
- Pack tactics for predators
- Territorial behavior
- Day/night activity patterns
- Weakness to specific elements

### 6. Day/Night Cycle

**Day (Dual Suns - 20 hours)**
- Safer exploration
- Better visibility
- Some creatures sleep
- Extreme heat in certain biomes
- Solar power optimal

**Night (12 hours)**
- Bioluminescent flora activates
- Nocturnal predators emerge
- Reduced visibility
- Temperature drops
- Unique resources available
- Beautiful but dangerous

### 7. Weather System

**Weather Types**
- Clear: Normal conditions
- Acid Rain: Damages health and equipment
- Electrical Storms: Dangerous but powers devices
- Spore Clouds: Reduces visibility, can be toxic
- Meteor Showers: Danger + rare materials
- Aurora Winds: Cold + navigation interference

**Impact**
- Affects visibility and movement
- Creates strategic opportunities
- Requires shelter or protection
- Some weather enables unique crafting

### 8. Progression Systems

**Character Skills**
- Survival: Resource efficiency, faster crafting
- Combat: Damage, accuracy, stealth
- Science: Better scanning, research speed
- Engineering: Advanced crafting, base efficiency
- Athletics: Stamina, movement speed

**Technology Tree**
- Unlock blueprints through research
- Find schematics in ruins
- Experiment with alien materials
- Reverse-engineer found technology

### 9. Story Progression

**Optional Objectives**
- Repair communications array
- Investigate other crash sites
- Explore ancient alien ruins
- Uncover planet's mysteries
- Find alternate ways off-planet

**Multiple Endings**
- Rescue: Signal successfully, get rescued
- Adaptation: Choose to remain on planet
- Discovery: Unlock ancient alien technology
- Sacrifice: Save others at personal cost
