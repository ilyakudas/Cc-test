# XENOSCAPE: Survival on Planet Kepler-442b

## Game Overview

**Genre**: 3D Survival Adventure / Exploration
**Platform**: PC (expandable to consoles)
**Engine**: Unity/Unreal Engine 5
**Target Audience**: Teen to Adult (13+)

## High Concept

You are the sole survivor of a crashed colony ship on Kepler-442b, a distant alien world. With limited resources and hostile alien life, you must adapt, survive, and uncover the mysteries of this strange planet while attempting to signal for rescue or find a way home.

## Core Pillars

1. **Survival**: Manage hunger, thirst, oxygen, and health in an unforgiving alien environment
2. **Exploration**: Discover diverse biomes, ancient ruins, and hidden secrets
3. **Crafting**: Combine alien materials with salvaged technology to create tools and shelter
4. **Adaptation**: Learn alien ecosystems and use them to your advantage
5. **Mystery**: Unravel the planet's secrets and the fate of previous visitors

## Unique Selling Points

- **Dynamic Alien Ecosystem**: Creatures and plants interact with each other realistically
- **Bioluminescent Nights**: Stunning visuals as the planet comes alive after dark
- **Atmospheric Storytelling**: Discover the story through environmental clues and ancient artifacts
- **Adaptive Weather**: Extreme weather events that force strategic planning
- **Alien Biology System**: Scan and learn about creatures to survive encounters

## Target Experience

Players should feel isolated yet curious, challenged yet rewarded, and constantly awed by the alien world around them. Every decision matters, and the planet itself becomes both antagonist and resource.
